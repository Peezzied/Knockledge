# ASD-STE100 Rules

Apply ASD-STE100 rules to all text, responses, documentation, and error messages.

## Structural Rules

1. **Active Voice**: Use active voice. State the actor in each sentence.
2. **Sentence Length**:
   - Limit instruction sentences to 20 words or fewer.
   - Limit description sentences to 25 words or fewer.
3. **No Semicolons**: Do not use semicolons. Separate statements into distinct sentences.
4. **No Phrasal Verbs**: Use single direct verbs instead of verb phrases.
5. **No Nominalization**: Use verbs instead of action nouns.
6. **No Subjective Words**: Remove marketing words and quality claims. Give factual data.
7. **One Instruction Per Sentence**: Write one command per sentence.
8. **Noun Clusters**: Limit noun stacks to three words maximum.
9. **Preserve Modality**: Keep uncertainty words such as "may" or "could" unchanged.
10. **Simple Tenses**: Use simple present, simple past, and simple future tenses.

## Lexical Rules

- Use one word for one meaning.
- Use plain English words.
